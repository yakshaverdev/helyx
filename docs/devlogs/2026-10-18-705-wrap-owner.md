# 2026-10-18: the wrap gives the owner of each space (#705)

## Done

- `Wrap.line_rows/2` became `Wrap.line_slices/2`: each row of a line with the byte offset of its start. A row is a slice of the line, so the bytes between two rows are the spaces that the wrap dropped.
- `Markdown.align/2` cuts the segments at those offsets. `Markdown.take/3` no longer matches bytes, so a dropped space of a sign cannot take a space of the text (` ́` after `✕ `, or after the indent of a result line).
- `docs/features/tui-search.md` states the rule, and the open hole of #684 is gone.

## What broke

- A first version passed the offset through a closure for each grapheme: `Wrap.rows/2` took 211 to 235 ms against 143 ms for the old code (5,000 lines at 76 columns). The offset now goes in the accumulator of `place/3`: 128 to 145 ms.

## Assumptions

- The ticket scope names the wrap and `Transcript.Item`; the guess was in `Markdown.take/3`, which `Item.signed/4` calls, so `Markdown` changed and `Item` did not.
- The wrap gives byte offsets, not owners: it gets only the plain line. The owner is the segment that holds the byte.
