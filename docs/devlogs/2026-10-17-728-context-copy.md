# 2026-10-17: the copies of the transcript at each provider round (#728)

Done:

- A send trace showed which sends copy the whole transcript at each prepare. The supervisor hop that the ticket named first is not a copy (see "Trace before").
- The prepare Task gives back the messages that the plugins kept as `:base`, and the session puts in the list that it gave the Task. `Turn.prepare` holds `{pid, messages}`. This removes one of the three copies of each prepare. The kill bound of the Task and the check of `Helyx.Session.Stream.prepare/4` do not change: the check runs before the swap, in the Task.
- A test in `test/helyx/session/provider_process_test.exs` (a steer that joins while the context builds) checks that the context holds the messages that the plugins saw, not the transcript of the reply time. A probe that put in `state.transcript` made that test fail (`left: "more"`, `right: "hold_prepare"`).

## Trace before

Elixir 1.19.5, `Task.Supervisor.async_nolink/3` (`lib/task/supervisor.ex:610`): the call to the supervisor carries only `[get_owner(owner), :monitor]`. The owner then sends `{owner, alias, alias, callers, {module, fun, args}}` to the new Task, one send. Thus the supervisor never gets the closure, and the "two 5-tuples that start with the session pid" of #669 are the starts of two prepare Tasks: the turn start and the context request.

Probe (`/tmp`, not in the repository): the bench setup at 30,000 messages, one warm-up batch, then an `:erlang.trace/3` `:send` trace of the session process and of new processes over one batch, with `:erts_debug.flat_size/1` of each message. Sends over 10,000 words, without the two replies to the `:sys.get_state/1` calls of the bench, which are outside the timed part:

| Send | 1 x 8 batch | 4 x 8 batch |
|----|----|----|
| session to a new prepare Task (the 5-tuple start) | 2, about 1.83 M words each | 5 |
| prepare Task to the session (`{ref, {:prepared, ...}}`) | 2 | 5 |
| session to the provider process (`:provider_request`: `:turn` and `:context`) | 2 | 5 |
| all the other sends | 145 sends, 4,777 words | 499 sends, 16,591 words |

A batch of `n` rounds has `n + 1` prepares (the turn start and one context request after each round), and each prepare makes three copies.

## Trace after

The same probe on the change: the `:prepared` replies hold no transcript and are under 10,000 words. The starts and the provider requests stay, 2 and 2 in a 1 x 8 batch, 5 and 5 in a 4 x 8 batch. Other sends: 147 sends, 4,909 words (1 x 8); 504 sends, 16,921 words (4 x 8).

I did not measure the garbage collections, so this cause is not proved.

## Decision

- The supervisor hop: no change, it is not a copy.
- The reply of the prepare Task: the change removes its copy when the plugins keep the message list. `Helyx.ModelContext.Default` keeps it, and `Helyx.Compaction.Summary` keeps it until the first summary. After a summary, the reply holds the summary and the kept messages, which are fewer.
- The start of the prepare Task: it stays. The plugins run in the Task so that its kill bounds their time, and the Task needs the messages.
- The send to the provider process: it stays. It is the context request of `Helyx.Provider.Loop` (ADR 0002), out of scope for this ticket. After the change, it and the Task start are the two copies left in each prepare. See "Next".

Assumptions (owner away):

- The session keeps the list that it gave the Task, not its transcript at the reply. A provider event can append to the transcript while the Task runs, for example the `user_message` of a steer or a tool result. The context must hold what the plugins saw. The new test covers it.
- `Turn.prepare` holds `{pid, messages}`, not a new field, so the clears of `prepare` also drop the list.
- I measured the change on `94618cf4`, then rebased it on `e7a3d6f6` and `6b3ababb`. They add the bench (#669), test fixes (#724, #730), and a Memory sync fix (#721). None of them changes the prepare path.
