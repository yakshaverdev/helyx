## `erl_child_setup: failed with error 32 on line 284`

Harmless. It shows in 19 of the 27 logs in `precommit-fails/` on 2026-10-15, all in the `plugins/bundled` tests. In `erl_child_setup.c` (the same in OTP 28.0, 28.3, and 28.5.0.6), line 284 is the read of the emulator's ack in the forked child, before `exec`. `read_all/3` sets `EPIPE` (32) at end of file. The emulator sends the ack when the port reads the child's pid; if the port closed first, the child reads end of file, prints the line, and exits before it runs the program. Nobody waits for that program: its port is closed.

Reproduction: stop the VM's `erl_child_setup` with SIGSTOP, let a process block in `Port.open/2`, kill that process (or halt the VM), then send SIGCONT. The line appears, the VM lives on, and `/bin/echo` does not run. In the test runs, a process killed while it opens a port does this, for example a Task killed with `:brutal_kill` in `System.cmd/3`.
