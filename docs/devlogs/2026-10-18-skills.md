## Measurement

The scan of one folder with 200 skills of 2,046 bytes each, each with a folded description, 20 runs after one warm-up run, on the development machine:

- with `yaml_elixir`: median 65.5 ms, max 74.2 ms;
- with the frontmatter reader, at a load average of 5 to 7: median 56.5 ms (max 282.0 ms) in one run and 22.6 ms (max 46.2 ms) in a second. The two runs differ; the cause was not measured.

`Helyx.Skills.Frontmatter.read/1` on 64,000 bytes of each worst shape (doubled quotes, blanks, line breaks in a quote, folded lines, 64,000 `[` in a skipped key, 12,800 keys, ` #` runs, `\x41` escapes, an open quote): at most 13.6 ms, and each shape took at most 2.5 times its time on 32,000 bytes. A review rerun on the loaded machine measured 14.7 ms. Not measured on the precommit host.

The fail-closed reader on 64,000 bytes of ten worst shapes (12,800 keys, a long plain line, doubled quotes, `\x41` escapes, a folded block of 10,000 lines, quotes and brackets in another key, 32,000 nested brackets, comment lines, lines of spaces, a control character at the end), the median of 7 runs: at most 21.1 ms (the keys), and each shape took 1.8 to 2.2 times its time on 32,000 bytes. A probe of 100,000 generated frontmatters against `yaml_elixir` found no difference; about 13 % were accepted. After review round 1 (a byte trim, a tab after the indentation out), a rerun of 100,000 found no difference; about 10 % were accepted.

The rule of characters (the fourth `/ship` run, after simplify) on 64,000 bytes, the median of 7 runs: 10,666 other keys with `\r\n` line ends 24.9 ms, 12,800 other keys 20.2 ms, 16,000 short indented lines of another key 16.1 ms, 64,000 empty lines in a block 16.0 ms, 64,000 empty lines in another key 5.9 ms, 32,000 lines of a space 4.1 ms, one long line of another key 3.0 ms; each took 1.86 to 2.26 times its time on 32,000 bytes. A probe of 100,000 generated frontmatters against `yaml_elixir` found no difference; 6,596 were accepted (after review round 1).

## What broke

- Review round 1 found three ways that one `SKILL.md` broke every scan: a `!!binary` description that is not UTF-8 raised in the catalog, YAML aliases expanded a short frontmatter past the memory of the node, and deep nesting parsed for 30 s. A parse process with a heap bound and a deadline fixed them, and round 2 found two more defects in that process (the copy of the result lost its sharing, and the process outlived a killed caller); precommit then dropped a valid skill at the deadline on a loaded host. The orchestrator removed the premise: the YAML library is gone, and a reader of only `name` and `description` runs in the caller, linear in the bytes (`docs/reviews/2026-10-18-skills.md`).
- The three rounds of the second `/ship` run found 16 reproduced defects, all but two in the YAML fidelity of the reader. Round 3 still found 5, and the main one (a quoted value of a skipped key that goes on at column 0 can hide a `name` key) is no small fix, so it is open with four smaller ones (findings 32 to 36 of the review record).
- The orchestrator changed the premise again: the reader fails closed and is proved against `yaml_elixir` by a differential property. The property found three places where `yaml_elixir` differs from YAML 1.2 (it reads `+`, `0x`, and `+0x1` as numbers, gives other last line breaks for `>+`, and rejects a plain value that starts with `:[`); the subset leaves them out.
- The quote and bracket scan of the other keys failed in four rounds (findings 32 and 39 to 41): each time it took a quote that YAML opens for text, or the reverse, because it had to find where a YAML value ends. The orchestrator deleted it. A rule of characters replaced it: in another key, a character that starts a YAML construct is out at the start of a word.

## Assumptions

- A broken skill does not hide a farther skill of its name; a clash is between skills that load.
- A skill that the catalog cut leaves out is unknown to the tool and to the user command too, so the three agree.
- A user command always expands to the full text, also after an earlier load: the client holds no transcript.
- The load text keeps the frontmatter, so the line numbers of a cut match `SKILL.md`.
