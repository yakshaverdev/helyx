# Review: one generic answer for Codex output out of order (#365)

Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `origin/master` at `f8092d1`.

Invariant: `Helyx.Provider.Codex` takes the program's lines only through `init/3` (the handshake) and `info/2`, through `Helyx.Provider.Codex.Check.malformed/2`. Each answer applies only to the request with its id. Every line outside the observed order stops the provider process or fails the connect, with no repair or buffering: a `turn/started` or `turn/completed` before or without the `turn/start` answer, a tool item's `item/completed` with no open item, a message close or a taken steer while a call of a closed message runs, a `subAgentActivity` item of another kind than `completed` with the id of a turn that is not running, and an error answer to `initialize` or `thread/start`. Accepted: a duplicate `item/started` of a tool item gives a second tool call, a completion with another type than its start closes the item, a `turn/interrupt` answer after the turn's end is dropped, and the open steer requests (`asked`) outlive the turn. Kept with evidence: `agents` and `:agent_running` (#226), `:command_running`, `:tool_running`, `:item_of_ended_turn` (#198), the request-id table, the pending interrupt until the turn id is known, the exact steer error, the resume digest, the lost-thread error, and the line cap.

Without it, the session gives a running call `aborted` and drops its real result: data loss, which the rule of #360 keeps as safety.
