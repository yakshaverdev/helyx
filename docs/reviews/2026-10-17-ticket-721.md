# Review: a log line for every memory sync loss (#721)

Scope: `Helyx.Session.Sync` (`notice/3`, `terminate/2`, the drain end); `docs/features/memory.md`.

Invariant: every chunk that the sync worker drops gives a log line with its counts, whether by a failed call, a dropped live batch, or a stop of the worker with a queue left (the drain limit, the drain cap, a Core stop). `notice/3` always logs its text, and also sends it to the session until the worker handles the session `:DOWN`; `terminate/2` writes the drain-end line of #714 once when the queue is not empty. Accepted holes: a live failure writes two log lines (the reason of `Helyx.Reason.log/2` and the notice text); a kill of the sync supervisor (unchanged from #707); a kill of Core's task supervisor while a worker starts a call; a worker still busy at its shutdown limit of 1,000 ms.
