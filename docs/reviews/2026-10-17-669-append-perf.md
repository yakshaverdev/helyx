## Invariant

The bench measures each part that the ticket names (append, call matching, resume fold, file append of 1 KiB and 64 KiB entries, JSON encoding, and Fake provider batches with persistence on and off at 1k, 10k, and 30k messages) with time, reductions, and heap, and each number in the devlog comes from a bench run or a named probe. Accepted holes: the batch runs with no ModelContext or Compaction plugin; the heap of a part counts only the retained words of its result; the runs share the machine with other worktrees and were not made on the precommit host; the per-round context copy is left for a follow-up ticket.
