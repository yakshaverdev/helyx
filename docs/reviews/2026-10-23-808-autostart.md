The two `wire_test.exs` failures had two causes. The frame at the frame bound (`:152`): the fake server sent the frame over the bound with `:ok = :gen_tcp.send`, and the client closes at its header, so the send can fail; the send is now `_ =`, and the asserts of the client stay. The pings past the deadline (`:231`): a deadline of 20 ms could end before the hello under load; the deadline is now `@load_ms` (500 ms), and the server sends pings until the client closes, so a read past the deadline never ends and the test fails at the ExUnit timeout, as it did with the finite stream and the welcome.

The `fold` helper of `tui_test.exs` gave its fake events `state.vm.seq + 1`, so the events of the real session of the test fell at or below the `seq`; with the fix of row 1 the scroll test lost its branch read. The helper puts the `seq` back after each fake event.
