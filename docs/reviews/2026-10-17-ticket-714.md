# Review: a cap on the memory sync drains (#714)

Scope: `Helyx.Session.Sync` (the session `:DOWN`, `workers/1`, `drain_end/1`, `notice/3`), `Helyx.Core.max_sessions/0`; `docs/features/memory.md`.

Invariant: a sync worker whose session ends starts its drain only while the sync supervisor of Core links at most the session cap plus 64 workers (128), counted from the links of the supervisor with no call to it; past that it drops its queue at once with the log line of the drain end, which gives the count of chunks and of unsaved messages. So at most 128 workers drain at once, each under its queue bound, and a session start never waits or loses its sync for the drain of another session. The newest drain is dropped, not the oldest. Accepted holes: a kill of the sync supervisor (unchanged from #707); in a Core stop the supervisor unlinks its children before their shutdown, so a count in that window can start a drain past the cap until the shutdown that comes next.
