# Review of #885: UI block models in the Rust core and the GPUI app

Fast mode (orchestrator, 2026-11-09): one round, the Codex adversarial review and the failure-path agent in parallel. Base `eeccca9c`, the working tree with `clients/rust/core/src/vocabulary.rs` and `clients/rust/core/tests/vocabulary.rs` untracked.

Invariant: every UI block that the Rust core holds (the `ui_blocks` of a tool result, a shown block of the snapshot or of a `ui_block` event, and the request of an ask with a kind) reads into a model of `vocabulary` with no panic: `form` and `approval` natively when their fields are within the bounds of `docs/features/ui-blocks.md`, any other kind or fields as the fallback text, with a cut mark; the app draws each under its result, under the oldest open call of its call id, or at the end of the turn, within the drawn bounds of the TUI.

## Round 1

### Codex

Verdict approve, no findings.

### Failure path

| # | Finding | Resolution |
| - | ------- | ---------- |
| 1 | Reproduced: an ask of kind `approval` with a `text` of 900,000 bytes, a `tool` and a `provider` of 50,000 bytes each passes the core (the frame is under 1,048,576 bytes), and the app drew all three whole; only the arguments had the bound of the TUI. The core does not check the 65,536 bytes of an ask request. | Fixed: `vocabulary::head` cuts a drawn text at a character boundary; the app draws the title at most 1,024 bytes on one line, the tool and the provider at most 256 bytes each, the arguments at most 16,384, and the text of any other block at most 65,536 bytes with the `[cut]` mark. Test: `tests/vocabulary.rs`, "a drawn text keeps its head within the bound". |
| 2 | Measured: each redraw reads the model of every UI block of the transcript under the lock, 10 to 21 ms for 200 results of 8 blocks of 65 KiB. | Accepted, written in the row "the redraw of the window" of `docs/features/client-kit.md`: the redraw already copied the text of every row under the lock, and the transcript has the bound of the TUI. |
| - | Not a defect: `UiBlock::read` checks the envelope again, which every holder of a block checked. | Kept: `read` is public, so a host can call it with any value, and the check is the envelope only (no walk of the size). |

Held, per the agent: no frame that passes the core makes `UiBlock::read` fail, so the `expect` of the app cannot fire; the form and approval reads match `picker?/1` and `readable?/1` of the TUI; `open_calls` gives the oldest open call; a key that a JSON object repeats takes its first pair everywhere.

Not reached: the GPUI window was not launched; no fuzzing of random blocks.

No rerun round: fast mode.
