# Review: #710, the switch stops the old session with `Helyx.Sessions.stop/1`

Scope: `git diff HEAD` on branch `ticket/710-tui-switch-stop` (base `c17f35a4`). The guard clause of item 2 was already on master (#720). This diff makes the two other `tui.ex` rewrites (393 lines now) and moves `Switch` to `Helyx.Sessions.stop/1`.

## Equivalence of the stop

The old stop called `Session.abort/1`, then `GenServer.stop(pid, :shutdown, :infinity)` in a catch of every exit. `Helyx.Sessions.stop/1` calls the same abort and `GenServer.stop(pid, :normal, :infinity)`:

- The abort waits with `:infinity`, and `Helyx.Session.Call.call_pid/3` turns every exit but a timeout into `{:error, :session_not_found}`, so no exit leaves it.
- `GenServer.stop/3` wraps each exit, `:noproc` too, as `{reason, {GenServer, :stop, args}}`, and `Sessions.stop/1` catches both shapes. `Session.pid/1` rescues the `ArgumentError` of a Core that is gone.
- The reason `:normal` against `:shutdown`: `Server.terminate/2` ignores it, `Session.end_reason/1` gives `:stopped` for both, and the TUI drops the end signal of the old session.
