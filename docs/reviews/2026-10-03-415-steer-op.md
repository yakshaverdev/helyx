# Review: accepting a steer is one operation (#415)

Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `b56b166`. Scope: `lib/helyx/session/queue.ex`, `server/steering.ex`, `server/turn_loop.ex`, `long-lived-harness.md`.

## Invariant

A client steer on a turn or a wait is accepted only through `Steering.steer/2` (called by `TurnLoop.admit/3`): in the `submitted` and `context` phases it checks that the queued plus sent steers are under 32 before it sends the steer to the provider process with one request and records it in the `Queue` ledger, and in `preparing`, `submitting`, and a wait `Queue.push/3` checks the limit and queues the steer, so the 32-steer bound holds on both paths, a full queue answers `queue_full` with no send and no state change, a steer is sent at most once per acceptance, steers queued in `submitting` go out once at the turn `:ok` and keep their place, a confirmed rejection queues the steer for the next turn, and an unconfirmed steer is never sent again. Accepted: `Queue.room?/1` and the unchecked `Queue.sent/4` stay public in the pure `Queue`, because the request to the provider process cannot run there; the private `Steering.send_steer/2` is the only caller of `Queue.sent/4` in `lib/`.
