## Round 1 (full)

- Simplify: the delete operations call the Hub directly; one shared `Access.wire/1`; shared `call/4` and `rows/3` in `Access.Subscriptions`; removed `secret_text/1`, `new_id/0` (a copy of `Schedules.new_id/0`), and two identity functions in the tools; one DDL attribute for the `subscriptions` table; merged list and create clauses in `server.ex`.
- Codex, spec: a wrong or missing signature of a known id computed two HMACs, an unknown id one, against the one-HMAC rule of decision 17.
- Failure path (reproduced), spec: a put-back (`turn_running`, `queue_full`) wrote a nil `last_outcome` and a new `last_at` to the subscription, so `delivered` disappeared from the listing.
- Codex (reproduced in memory): a later outcome of another pending delivery replaced `refused`, and the endpoint answered 202 again. With the previous finding, two findings on one mechanism: the brief fixes the write of the outcome to the subscription, not each path. Only a final outcome writes, and `refused` stays until a delete.
- Codex (reproduced in memory): the pending count read rows with a `delivery_id`, which the decide step clears before the session call, so a webhook accepted in that window and a put-back made 17 pending rows. A row now counts as pending until it has a final outcome.
- Spec: `subscription_list` had no output budget; 16 templates of 16 KiB of one session pass `Helyx.Message.max_result_bytes/0`. Fixed as `schedule_list`: a cut template, a 60,000-byte budget, a last line that counts the rest.
- Standards: `hub.ex` grew to 426 lines; `subscribe/3`, `subscriptions/3`, and `unsubscribe/3` had no `safe/1`, so an exit of a Hub call crashed the caller. They go through `Helyx.Hub.Api` as the schedule functions do.
- Standards: `Webhook.render/2` public; the tool wrote the path itself; a test comment at the prompt bound that no test proved. Fixed.
- Spec, standards: guarantees with no test: a label with a control character, a path id over 64 bytes, 404 after a delete, the migration (`user_version` 2, a reopen, schedule rows kept), the prune after two hours, the expiry of the rolling hour. Tests added.
- Spec: the ticket names an optional filter. Not built: the sender chooses its events, and the agent reads the payload and can ignore it. Out of scope in `hub.md`.
- Standards, rejected: one keep-alive connection could hold the 16 places of the webhook gate. Two probes showed that the listener closes the connection after one request (`max_requests: 1`).
- Standards, not changed: both `Access.Subscriptions.webhook/3` and the transport catch an exit. The transport catch is the boundary of any authority; the Hub one gives the documented 503 of a Hub that does not answer.
- Accepted after the fix: the decide step writes `unconfirmed` before the send, so the cap counts a delivery that ended `unconfirmed` until its prune (`hub.md`, Accepted holes).

## Round 2 (reduced)

The briefs named the five invariants of the round 1 fixes and asked for another path that breaks each.

- Failure path: no defect. Probes: one HMAC for an unknown id (with and without a header), a missing header, a wrong signature, an empty body, a duplicated header, upper-case hex, and a `refused` subscription with a right signature and with none; 0 HMACs for a path id over 64 bytes and for a body that is not UTF-8, for known and unknown ids alike; `{:error, :timeout}` from `subscribe/3`, `subscriptions/3`, and `unsubscribe/3` with a suspended Hub and with a dead Hub pid, with no late reply in the mailbox. By reading: only `settle/5` writes the subscription, on final outcomes; both states of a row in flight are in the cap count; a row in flight outlives no prune (3,600 s life plus a 120,000 ms Task against a 7,200 s prune).
- Spec, test: the put-back test waited for a retry time that the start of the Task had already written, so it did not prove the put-back. It now waits for a row with no outcome and a retry time at most 30 s ahead. Passed 3 of 3.
- Spec, test: the HMAC count test now covers a `refused` subscription.
- Spec, by reading, not reproduced: a delivery Task killed at its limit or crashed after the decide step leaves the subscription without its `unconfirmed`. Recorded in `hub.md`, Accepted holes.
- Spec, wording: the listing's `pending` (a delivery waits to be sent) and the cap's pending count differ; decision 17 and the Bounds row now say which is which. A path id over 64 bytes and a method other than POST answer 404 with no HMAC; decision 17 now says so.
- Spec, not changed: the timeout test stops the Hub, so the exit is caught in `api_arg/1`, not in `Api.safe/1`; the failure path probe with a suspended Hub reached `safe/1`.

The round reproduced no defect, so the loop ends.

## Accepted

The holes of `hub.md` for #921: the secret in the store, the time of a refusal, no delivery history, no `Retry-After`, a refused subscription without a run, a delivery row as a schedule row, the Hub process reads each webhook body, an `unconfirmed` delivery holds its place, a delivery Task that dies after the decide step.
