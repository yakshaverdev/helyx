Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `c46649a`. Scope: `plugins/bundled/lib/helyx/harness_io.ex`, `plugins/bundled/lib/helyx/provider/codex.ex`, `plugins/bundled/lib/helyx/watchdog.ex`, `plugins/bundled/test/support/watchdog_harness.ex`, their tests, `coding-agent.md`, `long-lived-harness.md`, and `docs/agents/review-checklist.md`.

## Invariant

The harness port stays linked to the provider process, its owner, a Task of the hands that does not trap exits. A write to a watchdog that died while the port is open closes the port with `:epipe`, and that exit signal ends the provider process, also during a close or the Codex handshake. The hands report `{:provider_down, pid, {:task_exit, :epipe}}` after the release of the watchdog and command groups (ADR 0004), the session fails the turn and lives on. The port closes with its owner however the owner ends. No handler for `:epipe` stays. Accepted hole: program output still in the mailbox of the provider process is not read, and a terminal there is lost (`coding-agent.md`, row "bash command start after the group marker").
