# Review: the wrap gives the owner of each space (#705)

Scope: the working tree against `HEAD` (`c2318752`), the branch point: `Helyx.TUI.Wrap` (`line_rows/2` becomes `line_slices/2`), `Markdown.align/2` and `take/3`, three test files, `docs/features/tui-search.md`, and one name in `docs/features/coding-agent.md`. `origin/master` moved ahead during the work (#761); the change does not touch those files. Feature doc: `docs/features/tui-search.md`, "The search rows".

Invariant: each row of `Wrap.line_slices/2` is the slice of its line at the byte start it states, and `Markdown.align/2` gives each byte of a row the tag of the segment that holds that byte, so a space that the wrap drops at a row break never moves to another segment, and a dropped sign space never takes a space of the text.

## Reproduction

`search_test.exs`, "a space of the text after a dropped sign space stays search text", on the code before the fix: a notice `" ́"` and 20 letters at 20 columns has no mark for `" ́a"`. The second case of the test, a result line `" ́abcdef"` at 4 columns, is the result-line case of the old open hole.

## Decision

The guess was `Markdown.take/3`, which `Transcript.Item.signed/4` reaches through `Markdown.segment_rows/2`. So the change is in the wrap and in `Markdown`, and `Transcript.Item` does not change. The wrap knows only the plain line, not the sign, so it gives the byte start of each row; the owner of a dropped byte is the segment that holds it, derived in `align/2` and not stored.

Cost: the offset is in the accumulator of `place/3`, with no extra call for each grapheme, and a row is a `binary_part/3` of the line. Measured on 2026-10-18, `Wrap.rows/2` on 5,000 lines of 168 bytes at 76 columns, best of 5: 143 ms before, 128 to 145 ms after. A first version with a closure for each grapheme took 211 to 235 ms and was replaced.
