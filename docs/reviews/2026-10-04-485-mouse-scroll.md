# Review: wheel scroll, drag selection, and OSC 52 copy (#485)

Date: 2026-10-04. Base: `origin/master` fd01e2c, then 959c880 after the rebase on #484, #486, and #487.

Invariant (after the rebase): mouse events enter only through one clause of `Helyx.TUI.handle_event/2`, which hands them to `Helyx.TUI.Selection.handle/3` with the rects of the frame. A wheel step moves the scroll position three rows under `Transcript.hold/4`; a step that moves nothing draws no frame. A mouse event never arms or disarms `Helyx.TUI.Quit`. A left press inside the drawn transcript rect starts a selection whose ends are transcript addresses with a column; a drag moves the end, clamped to the drawn rows; a release copies the selected text in one whole OSC 52 `IO.write/1`, only when the text is at most 75,000 bytes, else the footer shows `not copied: over 75 KB` and nothing is written. Accepted holes, in `docs/features/coding-agent.md`, "TUI": a frame drawn while the write runs can show broken cells until the next frame; a cell that changes its rows under a selection changes the selected text; an emoji sequence that the width rule over-counts (#90) moves the copied columns away from the highlight. ExRatatui turns mouse capture off in `restore_terminal` on every exit, as it does raw mode.

Coordinator decisions: OSC 52 goes out with `IO.write/1` to standard output, the method of the ExRatatui 0.14.1 guide, because the library has no clipboard call. `tui.ex` was over 400 lines until #484 moved the status bar out; after the rebase it adds no net line (400) and has no `.credo.exs` entry.

## Rebase on #484, #486, and #487 (reduced round: spec, failure path, Codex)

The coordinator asked for no net line in `tui.ex` (400 on master), one mouse clause that hands the event to `Helyx.TUI.Selection` as #486 does with `Helyx.TUI.Quit`, and no frame for a wheel step that does not move the view. Changes:

- `Selection.handle/3` takes the event, the state, and the rects of the frame, and returns the answer of `handle_event/2`. It owns the wheel, the reason clear of a press, the selection, and the copy. It compares the result with the state as it came in: an unchanged state draws no frame, a wheel step at an end included. This supersedes the reason clear in `Helyx.TUI` of round 2 and the `moved` clause.
- `layout/2` gives the transcript, composer, and footer rects, nil with no size, to `render/2`, `on_screen/2`, and the mouse clause; it replaces `inner_width/1`.
- The `:clipboard_fn` seam is gone: the copy calls `IO.write/1`, and the tests read it with `ExUnit.CaptureIO.with_io/1`.
- A wheel step on a transcript of no rows does nothing; PgUp still scrolls it as one row.

## Manual check (for a person, in Ghostty)

1. A drag in the transcript, then a paste in another program: the selected text arrives. This checks that `IO.write/1` reaches the terminal while ExRatatui holds it.
2. A drag of a long text while output streams: a frame drawn during the write can break cells until the next frame. If a frame lands inside the escape sequence, the copy itself fails; that is wider than the accepted hole and needs a ticket.
3. The wheel and the trackpad scroll the transcript, and the terminal gets its mouse back after Ctrl+C twice and after a crash.
