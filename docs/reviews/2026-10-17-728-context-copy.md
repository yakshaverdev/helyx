## Invariant

The context that reaches the provider (`:turn` and `:context` requests) is always the messages that the plugins saw, also when the transcript grows during the build. When the plugins kept the message list, the reply of the prepare Task holds `:base`, and the session puts back the list that it stored in `Turn.prepare` (`{pid, messages}`). The kill bound of the Task, its monitor and `:DOWN` path, the kills at the stop and at the turn end, and the check of `Helyx.Session.Stream.prepare/4` (in the Task, before the swap) do not change. Accepted holes: a stale `{:prepared, ...}` reply of a killed Task matches a later turn that reuses its turn id (a provider-started turn, #240, whose id the session does not check for reuse); this was so before the change, the reply now gets the messages of the current Task, and no probe reached it; the Task start and the provider request still copy the whole transcript (the context request of `Helyx.Provider.Loop`, ADR 0002, is out of scope); after a summary, and in a `{:summarize, plan}` reply, the reply still holds its messages; no test checks the size of the reply (the send trace in the devlog does).
