Scope: `Helyx.TUI.ViewModel` (`queue/1`, the `queue_update` clause, `from_snapshot/1`), `Helyx.TUI.Footer.widget/5`; the footer lines of `docs/features/coding-agent.md` and `docs/features/usage-and-limits.md`.

Invariant: the TUI view model keeps the five queue counts `steers`, `follow_ups`, `running`, `held`, and `waiting` from the snapshot and from each `queue_update` through one function, which crashes on a payload that lacks one of them and drops any other field, and row 2 of the footer shows each of `running`, `held`, and `waiting` that is not zero as `N running`, `N held`, `N waiting`, after the queued count and before `scrolled`, in the same dim style.
