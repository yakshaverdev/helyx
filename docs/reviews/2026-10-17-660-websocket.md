# Review: the WebSocket transport (#660)

Scope: `Helyx.Transport.WebSocket` and its modules under `plugins/bundled/lib/helyx/transport/web_socket/`, the conformance client `Helyx.Test.WireClient`, the Bandit and WebSock dependencies; `docs/features/wire-protocol.md`, ADR 0008.

Invariant: every client frame enters through `Connection.handle_in/2` and the upgrade plug, and each resource that a client can make the server hold has a bound that the feature doc states: 64 connections (HTTP 503), 1 MiB per inbound frame or fragmented message (close 1009), 25 MiB of joined parts (4413), 16 requests in flight (`busy`), 16 subscriptions, a 64 MiB outbound queue (4408), one encoder slot, the credits of each subscription (4408), the deadline of each op (`timeout`), and the hello, ping, and idle timers; the writers of a connection end with it.

Accepted holes: a ping cannot go between the parts of one frame; the close of a full queue is handled after the frames already queued; the 25 MiB join and its decode run in the connection process; an unsubscribed subscription lives until it handles its message; stdio is out of scope.
