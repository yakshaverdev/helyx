# Review: TurnLoop owns the turn lifecycle; the session owns the prepare Task (#386)

Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `0d8da9a`. Scope: `lib/helyx/session/server.ex`, the new `lib/helyx/session/server/turn_loop.ex`, `server/state.ex`, `server/stop.ex`, `turn.ex`, `hands.ex`, `.credo.exs`, `test/helyx/session/provider_process_test.exs`, `test/support/interfaces.ex`, `long-lived-harness.md`, and `one-provider-path.md`.

## Invariant

The session server matches each message to the current turn, call, or provider process, and `Helyx.Session.Server.TurnLoop` makes every `activity` and `conn` transition with the behaviour of master, except that the session owns the prepare Task: a child of the task supervisor that the session monitors, its pid in `Turn.prepare`, its armed kill the first act inside the fun, killed by the session at every turn end (also a normal end with a context request open) and at a session stop. A `:DOWN` before its context fails the turn or answers the open context request with `{:task_exit, reason}`. So a preparing turn no longer waits behind a hands release. An abort and a failure share one pipeline, and only an abort sends an interrupt. The session keeps its monitor of the provider process (#361). Accepted hole: after an untrappable kill of the session, the unlinked prepare Task lives until its armed kill at `prepare_ms`.
