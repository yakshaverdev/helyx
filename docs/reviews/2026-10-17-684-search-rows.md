# Review: the search rows of the TUI transcript (#684)

Scope: `Helyx.TUI.Search.marks/3`, `Transcript.find/5`, `Transcript.screen/3` (rows `{address, line, search}`), the new `Helyx.TUI.Transcript.Item` (the look of an item, moved from `Transcript`), `Markdown.rows/2` (the tag `:sign`) and the new `Markdown.segment_rows/2`, two patterns of `Selection`, and `docs/features/tui-search.md`.

Invariant: the row of a match and its marks come only from the search text that `Transcript.Item.rows/2` gives with each drawn row, never from a drawn sign (the bar, the fill, the `✕ `, the indent of a result line, the hint row, the awaiting row, an unknown-block placeholder, the Markdown prefix and its hang indent); a match that no drawn search text holds says `match not shown`.

Base: `bc23dc6c`, the merge base with `origin/master`. `origin/master` had moved to `94204fcf` with changes in `lib/helyx/session/` only, so the reviews used the merge base to see this change alone.

## Findings and resolution

Simplify, round 1:

- All four agents: `Search.marks/4` wrapped the matched item a second time and needed the view model, because `screen/3` dropped the search text that the wrap had made. Fixed: `screen/3` rows are `{address, line, search}`; `Search.marks/3` keeps its arity, `Transcript.search_rows/3` and the change in `tui.ex` are gone, and the frame wraps an item once.
- Skipped: the joined text of each Markdown row is built also with no open search (small next to the parse of `Markdown.rows/2`); the selection finds the bar by its style while the search uses the search rows (the selection copies what the user sees, on purpose).

Round 1:

- Spec and failure-path: the prefix that Markdown draws (`• `, a number, the indent of a code line, the hang indent) was search text, so ` b` marked a hang indent and `•` marked a bullet. Fixed: `Markdown` tags its prefix `:sign`, and `Item.markdown_rows/2` leaves leading sign segments out. Tests: `search_test.exs`, "the prefix of a Markdown item or code line and an unknown block are signs".
- Codex: at 1 column, the notice `"\u0301a b"` wraps as `✕`, ` \u0301`, `a`, `b`, and the space of the sign stayed search text on row 1. Fixed in round 1 by a carry of the sign into the next rows; that carry was wrong (round 2). Test: `search_test.exs`, "a sign space that a mark of the text joins on a narrow row is not search text".
- Spec: "no caller reads the drawn text of a row" was false (`Transcript.text/4` reads it for the selection). Fixed: "the search reads no drawn row".
- Standards: no test for the placeholder of an unknown block. Fixed: in the Markdown test above.
- Judgement, not changed: the type `t` of `Item` is `Message.t() | tuple()`; `Item.cut/3` lives in `Item` because it reads `@bar` (now named in the comment of the module); the name of the test of the hint row also covers the indent and the awaiting row.

Simplify, round 2:

- Three agents: `Item.signed/4` repeated the segment alignment of `Markdown.align/2` with a byte prefix. Skipped at first as a change outside the diff; round 2 then found two defects in the carry, and the round 2 fix uses the alignment of `Markdown`.

Round 2:

- Failure-path and Codex: the carry of the sign ate the first space of a later line, so `{:notice, 20 letters <> "\n foo"}` with ` foo` had no mark. Codex: at 2 columns, `"\u093Ex"` kept U+093E, joined with the sign space, as text. Two findings on one mechanism, so the mechanism changed: `Item.signed/4` wraps the sign and its text as tagged segments through `Markdown.segment_rows/2` (new). Tests: `search_test.exs`, "a sign space that the wrap drops does not hide text of a later line".
- Spec: a prefix as wide as the row had no test. Fixed: `markdown_test.exs`, `rows("1. x", 2)`.
- Standards (hard): "Replaced mechanism" did not name #684. Fixed. The word "sign" had two meanings; the feature doc now says "Markdown syntax" for the source signs. The data clump `{non_neg_integer(), String.t()}` is now `Item.search()`. `signs/1` is now `sign_rows/1`. The moduledoc of `Transcript` no longer names the internal `Item`.
- Failure-path: the `⚙ ` of a tool call line is search text. Decision, not changed: the call line is the text of the cell by the contract of #668, and a test types `⚙ bash`. Recorded in the feature doc.
- Spec and failure-path: Markdown draws `[a](u)` as `a (u)`, and the ` (` is search text. Accepted hole, in the feature doc: a sign in the middle of a row needs a list of text spans for each row. No ticket yet.

Round 3:

- Spec and Codex: when the wrap drops the sign space and the text starts with a space and a mark, `Markdown.take/3` gives the space of the text to the sign. Drawn text then has no search text, and the line says `match not shown`; no sign is marked. Reproduction: `{:notice, " \u0301" <> 20 letters}` at 20 columns, query `" \u0301a"`, `find/5` gives `{0, 0}` and `marks/3` gives `[]`. Open: recorded in the feature doc as an open hole. The fix gives the wrap the owner of each space.
- Spec: the `@doc` of `Markdown.segment_rows/2` does not state that the segments are one line of sanitized text, and a grapheme across two segments takes the tag of the first. Open, not changed after the last review.
- Spec probe: 32,000 random notices and results at 8 widths draw the same rows, spans, and styles as at `bc23dc6c`.
- Simplify, not applied: the lines after the first of a notice need no segments; `result_rows/3` runs `Wrap.sanitize/1` once for each line, not once for the result; the search text is built on every frame.
- Standards, judgement only: `Markdown.segment_rows/2` wraps segments that are not Markdown; `Markdown` is at 400 lines; the name `search` (a row) beside `search_text/1` (an item); the 3-tuple row.
