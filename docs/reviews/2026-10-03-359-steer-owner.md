# Review: one owner for the steer state (#359)

Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `origin/master` at `da4ff6a` (#360 merged). Scope: the new `Helyx.Session.Queue` (it replaces `Queues` and `Steers`), `Server.Steering` as its only writer, the steer parts of `Server`, `Wait`, `Turn`, and `State`, `.credo.exs`, and the feature docs.

## Invariant

The steer state of a session has one owner, the pure struct `Helyx.Session.Queue` in `State.queue` for the whole session, written only through `Helyx.Session.Server.Steering`. The user-visible steer rules do not change: a steer reaches the provider at most once; it is queued again only after a confirmed `:rejected` that comes before any notice for it; a steer with an `:ok` or unknown answer and no `user_message` gets one `:steer_unconfirmed` notice; an abort drops the queues, and no late `:rejected` after it queues anything; the 32 steers count the queued steers and the open ledger entries, and no caller passes a held count; no turn starts while a steer request is open. Accepted hole from #202: a `:rejected` during the turn can change the order against a later steer. Out of scope: the steer protocol, the armed kill of a steer request, and the tool call path (#358).

## Decisions taken alone

- The pure module is `Helyx.Session.Queue` and the field is `State.queue`, after the existing vocabulary (`queue_update`, `Snapshot.queue`, `:queue_full`). The applier keeps its name `Server.Steering`.
- Ledger states: three are kept (`sent`, `answered`, `settled`). `taken` and `noticed` became `settled`; `ended` became `sent` with the ledger's `turn_id` set (see the feature doc, "Built in #359").
- The wait end with an open steer request is checked in `Server.progress/1` (`Queue.open?/1`), not in `Wait.next/2`, so the signature of `Wait.next/2`, which #358 changes, stays.
- `queue_update` is derived: `Steering` emits it when the queue counts change, after the effects of the call.
- `Wait.after_turn/2` lost its `normal?` argument; `Wait.answer/4`, `abort/2`, and `provider_down/2` return the wait only.

## Simplify

- Reuse and altitude: the server read `state.queue.sent` in a guard. Fixed: `Queue.open?/1`.
- Simplification: `end_turn/3` repeated the abort rule for `sent` entries. Fixed: `end_turn(queue, id, false)` is the normal end followed by `abort/1`.
- Skipped: a shared limit helper for the two `push/3` clauses; one helper for the drain in `append_steers/1` and `send_local_steers/1`; one `apply` for the four one-line wrappers; `steer_open?` as an argument of `Wait.next/2` (#358 owns that function); the efficiency notes (`counts/1` twice per call, `++` in `map/2`): every list holds at most 32 entries.
