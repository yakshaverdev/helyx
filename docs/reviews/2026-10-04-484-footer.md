# Review: two-row footer, Home and End replace Ctrl+End (#484)

Date: 2026-10-04. Base: `origin/master` fd01e2c.

Invariant: the TUI footer is two rows under the composer: row 1 is the session directory (home as `~`) and the git branch, read only at mount and after each `turn_end` from the nearest `.git` at or above the directory (a directory, or a `gitdir:` file of a worktree or a submodule), with reads of at most 4,096 bytes of regular files only, and a character of Unicode category C or a byte that is not UTF-8 shown as `?`; row 2 is the model, the run state, the queue counts only when not zero, and `scrolled`, with no key hints, and a reject reason first in red. Home and End with an empty composer go to the oldest output (held by `Transcript.hold/4`) and the newest output, and otherwise move the composer cursor; Ctrl+End is gone. Entry points: `Helyx.TUI.run/1` with its new `:cwd` option, `handle_event/2`, `handle_info/2`, `Helyx.TUI.Footer.location/1` and `widget/4`. Accepted holes: a detached HEAD or a reftable repository shows no branch; `GIT_DIR`, `commondir`, and bare repositories are ignored; the read is on the disk of the TUI, right only in local mode; a branch change between turns shows after the next turn ends; a file that becomes a FIFO between the stat and the open, or a hung mount, blocks the TUI; tokens, cost, and context % come later (#488).

Checked before the build: ExRatatui 0.14.1 reports the keys `"home"` and `"end"` (`ExRatatui.Event.Key`, `session_input.rs`), its `Paragraph` does not wrap by default (`wrap: false`), and `Textarea` moves its cursor on `"home"` and `"end"`. The snapshot has no cwd, so the TUI takes `:cwd` from `CodingAgent` and not from Core.

## Mechanism change: git is the authority

After round 3, the orchestrator decided to delete the reader of `.git` and `HEAD`, because three rounds found defects in it. `Helyx.TUI.Footer.branch/1` now runs `git branch --show-current` in a task that starts at mount and after each `turn_end`. It keeps at most 256 bytes and gives git 2 s. The round 3 open items (the name `HEAD`, the trim, `ref:` spacing, the `gitdir:` test) are gone with the reader. The cwd in the snapshot: no change, by decision. Terminal.app Home and End: a manual check.
