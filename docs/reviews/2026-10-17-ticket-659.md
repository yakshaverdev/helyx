# Review: ticket #659, the replay ring, the atomic resubscribe, and the credits

Scope: `Helyx.Session.Ring` (new), `Helyx.Session.Server.Events` (subscribe,
replay, ack, the slow end), `Helyx.Session.Server` (the ack and `:DOWN`
clauses), `Helyx.Session.Call` and `Helyx.Session.History` (the `after`
option), `Helyx.Session.subscribe/2` and `ack/2`, the TUI and print mode (ack
and the slow end), `Helyx.Test.Burst` (new), and the docs `replay-ring.md`
(new), `session-subscribers.md`, `session-snapshot.md`, `end-signal.md`.

Invariant: the session keeps its newest 1,024 events and at most 4 MiB in a
ring, one subscribe call returns the events after `after` or a snapshot with
no event between the reply and the live events, and a subscription that
holds more than 16,384 unacked events gets `{:helyx_end, ref, :slow}` in
place of the next event and no event after it.

State at the commit: three rounds. Round 3 reproduced defects on the
mechanism that round 2 patched (the credit base of a repeated subscribe), so
no small-fix round applies (`/ship`, "One small-fix round after the limit",
"Two findings on one mechanism stop the patching"). The commit holds the code
of round 3; the open defects are under "Open".

## Open

The pid-level bound across subscriptions does not hold. Each of these lets a
pid that does not read its mailbox hold more than 16,384 events:

- A resubscribe with `after`: the client acks the replayed copies, and the
  cumulative ack also covers the unread live copies (22,650 in the mailbox
  after 25 resubscribes, no slow end).
- A failed subscribe sends `{:unsubscribe, pid}`, and the next subscribe takes
  a new base (45,018 after 3 rounds).
- A subscribe after a slow end takes a new base (32,770 after 2 turns).

No client of today does this: the TUI and print mode subscribe once for each
session, and a wire connection is one pid (#660). The decision is whether the
bound is for each subscription (remove the `min` of round 2 and state it) or
for each pid (keep the base past an unsubscribe and a slow end).
