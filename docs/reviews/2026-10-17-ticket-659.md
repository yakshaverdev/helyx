# Review: ticket #659, the replay ring, the atomic resubscribe, and the credits

Scope: `Helyx.Session.Ring` (new), `Helyx.Session.Server.Events` (subscribe,
replay, ack, the slow end), `Helyx.Session.Server` (the ack and `:DOWN`
clauses), `Helyx.Session.Call` and `Helyx.Session.History` (the `after`
option), `Helyx.Session.subscribe/2` and `ack/2`, the TUI and print mode (ack
and the slow end), `Helyx.Test.Burst` (new), and the docs `replay-ring.md`
(new), `session-subscribers.md`, `session-snapshot.md`, `end-signal.md`.

Invariant: the session keeps its newest 1,024 events and at most 4 MiB in a
ring, one subscribe call returns the events after `after` or a snapshot with
no event between the reply and the live events, and a subscription (each
subscribe starts one) that holds more than 16,384 unacked events gets `{:helyx_end, ref, :slow}` in
place of the next event and no event after it.

State: three rounds in the first commit. Round 3 reproduced defects on the
mechanism that round 2 patched (the credit base of a repeated subscribe), so
the ticket waited for a decision. The owner decision and round 4 are below;
round 4 reproduced no defect.

## Owner decision and round 4 (reduced: spec, failure path)

The orchestrator decided for the owner: the bound of 16,384 unacked events is
for each subscription, not for each process. A client that resubscribes and
does not read fills only its own mailbox, and the wire transport bounds its
own outbound buffer (#660). The `min(acked, base)` of round 2 went: each
subscribe starts with fresh credits. `replay-ring.md`, "The credits", states
the rule; the test "each subscription of a pid starts with fresh credits"
checks it. So the three open paths of round 3 are the documented behaviour.

Round 4 also covered the two precommit fixes (the `seq` in the ring, the
ring update for an event over 4 MiB).

Resolutions: "a subscriber holds" became "a subscription holds", and "per
subscriber" in `replay-ring.md` became "per subscription"; the base of a
replay is named `after` in both places. The wire doc and ADR 0008 still say
"per subscriber"; on the wire one subscription is one process, so #660
states it. A round with no reproduced defect ends the loop.
